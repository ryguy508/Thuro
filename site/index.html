About us

Local, family-run and hands-on

The people who quote your job are the people who show up to do it.

We run our own excavators and dump trucks out of Sterling, so there is no handoff to a stranger and no runaround when you call. Customers tell us the same thing again and again: we are organized, friendly and honest about what a job needs.

Whether it is a few hours of grading behind a house or a commercial lot that needs plowing every storm, we show up prepared and finish what we start. If the job is small, that is fine with us. Small jobs are welcome.

What we do

Services

  • Excavating

    Digging, grading, trenching, foundation holes, drainage and site preparation, done with our own excavators and dump trucks. We haul material in and out so you do not have to arrange a second company.

  • Septic and Title 5

    Excavation for septic system installation and repair, including work tied to Title 5 inspections. Call to talk through your property and what it needs.

  • Winter

    Snow plowing and removal

    Driveways, parking lots and business entrances cleared fast, residential and commercial. When snow piles up past what a plow can push, we remove it.

  • Winter

    Ice control for lots, walkways and driveways, so customers, employees and family can walk and drive safely after the plow goes through.
